Safety, Signals, and Stroller Adoption: Parental Decision-Making in the Dutch Stroller Market

You are being invited to participate in a research study titled "Safety, Signals, and Stroller Adoption: Parental Decision-Making in the Dutch Stroller Market." This study is being conducted by Gursahiba Dharni, a Master's student at Delft University of Technology (TU Delft), Faculty of Technology, Policy and Management, under the supervision of Dr Karolien van Nunen.

The purpose of this research is to understand how parents weigh safety relative to other factors such as usability, price, compatibility, and brand when choosing a premium compact stroller in the Netherlands. The findings will contribute to academic research in consumer safety and product adoption, and will be used for the completion of a publicly accessible Master's thesis at TU Delft.

If you agree to participate, you will be asked to complete a Q-sort activity (approximately 20-30 minutes), in which you rank approximately 50 short statements about stroller attributes on a grid from "most disagree" to "most agree." Your Q-sort responses will be collected and stored anonymously. No names or directly identifying information will be retained as part of the research data. Only basic demographic information will be collected (e.g., age range, number of children, whether this is your first stroller).

All data will be stored securely on TU Delft institutional systems, accessible only to the researcher and supervisors. Your rankings will be made public to support the associated MSc thesis.

Your participation is entirely voluntary. You may refuse to answer any question and may withdraw at any time without giving a reason and without any consequences. Because Q-sort data are collected anonymously, it may not be possible to remove your individual responses after submission.

If you have any questions about this study, please contact Gursahiba Dharni at G.K.Dharni@student.tudelft.nl or Dr Karolien van Nunen at K.L.L.vanNunen@tudelft.nl.

Thank you for considering participation in this research.

How the sort works

You will see 51 statements about premium compact strollers. Your job is to sort them into a pyramid grid from "most disagree" on the left (-5) to "most agree" on the right (+5).

The condition

As you sort each statement, ask yourself:

"How important is this factor in MY decision to buy a premium compact stroller?"

How to place a statement

  1. Click a statement from the pool at the bottom to select it.
  2. Click any empty slot in the grid to place it.
  3. Click a placed card to move it back to the pool, or click another slot to swap.

The grid

The grid has fixed column sizes (a fixed number of slots in each column). You must fill every slot to finish. The middle column (0) is for statements you feel neutral about.

Tip: Start by sorting roughly - place statements in any column on the side you broadly agree or disagree with. Then refine.
